# Enclosure

The enclosure has been designed with Onshape, a cloud based
cad system that is free of use (as far as you keep public 
your project). 
This enclosure's project is named: 

typeV2-third-battery-new_ventilation

And you can access the project and make modifications at your 
convenience. 

## Body
The main enclosure has two pieces: body and display-lid. 
There are also splitted versions (with notation left-right) in
case your printer bed size is smaller.

## Batery
The battery is hold with a small case: carrier and head. You 
should insert the battery negative contact in the carrier slit, 
and glue the battery positive contact in the head.

## Display
The display pcb is held on the lid with two clamps, that are 
screwed in the enclosure. 
There is space to insert the pcb (that holds the display with
double side tape) and also a thin acrylic sheet (from 0.25 to 
0.8 mm thickness).
